FaultyWx Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

FaultyWx is a private, invitation-only weather app operated by Lede Publishing LLC ("we"). It is distributed to a small group of invited testers through Apple TestFlight and is not offered to the general public. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the app itself is small: everything runs on our own server, and we do not sell data or run advertising.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in. When you sign in with Apple we receive your name and an email address (which may be Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your email). We use this to create and identify your account.
  • Location. With your permission, we use your location to show weather where you are and to set your home location. If you turn on background location, the app also records occasional coarse location updates ("breadcrumbs") from cell and Wi-Fi signals so that severe-weather alerts can follow you when you travel. Background location is optional and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Push token. If you enable notifications, Apple issues a device token that we store so we can deliver weather and alert notifications.
  • What you enter. Saved locations and app settings.
  • Operational logs. We log which weather data sources the app queried on your behalf, associated with your account, so we can monitor our own usage of upstream weather services.

How we use it

Solely to provide the app: current conditions, forecasts, radar, air quality, and severe-weather alerts personalized to your locations. We do not use your data for advertising, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is handled by a few service providers only to make the app work:

  • Apple - Sign in with Apple, and delivery of push notifications.
  • Cloudflare - sits in front of our server as a secure connection layer and processes request metadata in transit.
  • Weather data providers - to return a forecast, radar, or air-quality reading, we send location coordinates to third-party weather services. They receive the coordinates needed to answer the request, not your identity.

Where it is stored

Your account data, saved locations, and location breadcrumbs are stored on our own private server, not on a third-party cloud database. Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off background location at any time in Settings; the app keeps working for the locations you check manually.
  • Export your data. The app can export your profile and saved data on request.
  • Delete your account and data. You can delete your account from within the app, or email us to have your data removed. Deletion removes your profile, saved locations, and breadcrumbs from our server.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your associated data is removed from our server.

Children

FaultyWx is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page.
