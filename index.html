FaultyWx Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

FaultyWx is a private, invite-only weather app. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple - your name and email, used to create your account.
  • Location - to show local weather and to set your home. When you use "current location," we keep your most recent position and a short near-or-far-from-home record of your recent current-location checks, so severe-weather alerts stay relevant to where you actually are, even after you close the app. This follows from using current location and does not require the optional background setting below.
  • Optional background location - if you turn it on in Settings, alerts also follow you passively as you travel, even when the app is closed. It is off unless you enable it.
  • A notification token, if you enable alerts.
  • The locations and settings you save.

How we use it

Only to run the app: forecasts, radar, air quality, and severe-weather alerts for your locations. Nothing else.

Who else touches it

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. To make the app work, location coordinates go to third-party weather providers to fetch forecasts, Apple handles sign-in and notification delivery, and Cloudflare sits in front of our server. Everything else stays on our own server.

Your data

You can delete your account in the app, which erases your information from our server.
